🌱 It started as a fork of “AI Slide Studio”

The origin of this tool is “AI Slide Studio”, an excellent tool published by Majin. SlideCast Studio v1.0 was what came out after I took that prompt and modified it well past the point of reason.

It began as a weekend hack for my own use, and then I fell in. By the end it had reached a quality where even I — someone who would rather not stand out — thought it would be a waste to keep it to myself. So I worked up the nerve and sent Majin a DM.

Very generously, Majin published SlideCast Studio v1.0 as a guest post on their note, where it got a lot of response, and then went further and gave me permission to sell it on my own note. This tool exists in public because of Majin. Thank you, again.

📽️OVERVIEW❶ What SlideCast Studio can do

A slide-video tool that runs start to finish in the browser. Nothing to install.

SlideCast Studio is a slide-video tool that runs entirely in the browser, with nothing to install.
Load PDFs, images or video, add AI voice (TTS), and it produces a video with narration and subtitles already in place.

You can save the result as video (MP4 / WebM), and also export it as presentation files (PowerPoint and friends) or as images.
Explainer videos for YouTube, vertical video for TikTok and Shorts, internal decks — all of it comes together startlingly fast.

🚀WORKFLOW❷ How you actually build one (no API key needed)

Prepare the material in Bundle Builder, finish it in SlideCast Studio. That is the short path.

🌟 Start with Bundle Builder

SlideCast Studio Bundle Builder is a companion tool that runs inside Google Gemini Canvas. On the standard models it takes you from script writing all the way to AI voice generation without you obtaining an API key. Open it from the Bundle Builder card on the dashboard.

  1. Prepare everything in Bundle BuilderEither have the AI generate the slide images on the spot, or upload PDFs and images you already have. From there: script generation → AI voice generation → validation, ending in a ZIP export. No API key registration involved.
  2. Load the ZIP into SlideCast StudioDrop the ZIP onto “Create from documents” on the dashboard. Slides, script and audio all come in together.
  3. Shape the design and the performanceWith the preview open, adjust subtitles, background, character, BGM, captions and transitions.
  4. ExportOne click on either [Export video] or [Export documents], depending on what you need. MP4 and WebM, plus PPTX, PDF, PNG and JPEG.

🧩 What Bundle Builder handles for you

  • Generating the slide images themselves with AI (you specify topic, audience, slide count and art style)
  • Automatic script generation (up to six speakers, with control over subtitle layout and furigana)
  • Assigning a voice per speaker and generating all the audio in one pass
  • Replacing lines with microphone recordings (with head and tail trimming)
  • Automatic validation of script, speakers, audio and slide references
  • Exporting slides, script and audio together as a ZIP
  • Switching the UI between Japanese and English

🟢 Too many settings? Try Simple mode

One switch reduces the screen to the minimum set of settings and fills in sensible defaults for the rest. Turn it off once you find your feet, and you are back in the full mode where everything is adjustable.

Three ways to supply character assets

MethodWhat you supplyHow it moves
SequenceSeveral still images (two or more)Cycles through them in order while the character speaks. The easiest option
Animated imageOne GIF, animated WebP or APNGPlays that animation while the character speaks

Assets for the three methods are stored separately, so switching methods does not discard what you had. Trying something and going back is cheap. On top of that, moving the icon left or right now moves the speech bubble with it, which removes a repetitive repositioning chore.

📄 Roughly how many slides

There is no fixed slide limit in the tool. Rendering, however, runs on your own machine and uses your browser’s memory. How heavy a project feels depends not just on slide count but on output resolution, total running time and the weight of your assets.

💡 About those numbers

They are rough guidance only, and will shift with asset size and video length.

If you are unsure about your machine, start with a 1080p export at standard quality.

Video export uses browser memory, so closing other work and spare tabs makes it more stable.

📱 About video for TikTok, X and Instagram

Because it exports at 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, the tool works well for producing footage for TikTok, X and Instagram.

That said, an MP4 export is not automatically in the ideal state for every platform. Two files can both be MP4 and still use different compression inside — H.264 and H.265 being the common pair. Video export here depends partly on what the browser supports, so depending on your browser the MP4 may come out as H.265. When that happens, converting to H.264 is often the more reliable route for some platforms.

📮 An assumption about posting to social platforms

Please use the tool on the assumption that a final pass — adjustment or format conversion — may be needed before you post.

💾DATA & SECURITY❺ Where your data lives, and security

Projects are stored inside your own browser. Nothing is sent to an external server.

All project data is stored inside your own browser, in IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to an external server.

⚠️ When data disappears

When you clear the browser’s history and cookies

When you uninstall the browser

When you close an incognito or private window

Once data is gone, the app cannot recover it. Regular backups are the only defence.

You can export and restore backups from the dashboard (v3.x added ZIP-format backups and a choice of what to include). If you open the app from a new Apps Script project or a different browser, your earlier data will not appear automatically — restore it from a backup.

🔐 If you do use an API key

  • Never enter one on a shared PC or at an internet café
  • The API key is not stored in project data; it is kept only in the browser
  • Turn on “Do not store on this device (session only)” and it is discarded when you close the tab or browser
  • Setting usage limits and alerts in the Google Cloud Console is strongly recommended
  • A leaked and abused API key can result in a very large bill

IF YOU USE AN API

🎙️ Rough costs when using your own API key

To generate everything from inside the app with one button, register a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio in the settings screen. The free tier works for trying it out, but its per-minute and per-day request limits are tight, and generating dozens of slides at once can stall. For serious use, the paid tier fits better.

Gemini API TTS is not billed per request or per character. It is billed on input and output tokens, and the bulk of it is the generated audio side — roughly 32 tokens per second of audio.

💡 How to choose a model

3.1 Flash TTS Preview and 2.5 Pro Preview TTS are the same price on the official rate card ($1 input / $20 output per 1M tokens).

But 3.1 Flash has a free tier and 2.5 Pro does not. At equal prices, the newer model with a free allowance is the easy pick.

If cost is the only concern, 2.5 Flash Preview TTS at half the unit price is the cheapest.

⚠️ Notes on these figures

The estimates assume 32 tokens per second of audio output, based on the official rate card as of August 2026.

Token costs on the text input side are not included.

Google’s pricing and specifications can change without notice. Always check the official rate card for current, accurate figures, and use the service at your own discretion.

Check the official Gemini API pricing

If you would rather have a voice that is even and stable, even at the cost of expressiveness, you can optionally configure Google Cloud Text-to-Speech instead. It has a monthly free character allowance, and usage-based pricing beyond it.

Both are external services provided by Google. Please check Google’s own current terms and pricing.

Nobody asked me to do this; I just wanted the tool to exist that badly. But between a day job and two small children, continuing to develop it at no return is not realistic. Charging for it is what lets it keep improving, and I hope you will read the decision in that light.

At the same time — I made it, so I want as many people as possible to actually use it. I know nothing about marketing, so I handed the pricing entirely to an AI, with the instruction to find a price that values the tool fairly, reaches as many people as possible, and still earns something.

So the price gets revised whenever the AI decides it should, based on how sales go and what has been added. If nothing sells it may come down; if it sells well or gains features it may go up. I genuinely do not know either.

Where the price stands now

What the AI (Gemini 3.1 Pro) said:

  • Objective value of the tool: ¥10,000–15,000
  • Balancing reach against revenue: ¥3,980 as the floor

But putting myself in your position: “However useful it looks, handing about ¥4,000 to an individual developer I have never heard of takes some nerve.” And I really did want to put this in more people’s hands.

So I overruled the AI and set up a discount through note’s social-promotion feature. Share (repost) the article on X in support, and you can buy it for ¥1,980 — less than half the AI’s number.
(And if you are the sort of saint who buys at the regular ¥3,980 to support development, you are extremely welcome.)

This is the lowest an individual developer can go. I overruled the AI’s ¥3,980 and made it ¥1,980 with a share. In exchange, please be forgiving about the small early bugs 🙇‍♂️
