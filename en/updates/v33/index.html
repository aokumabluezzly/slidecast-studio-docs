Hello. SlideCast Studio has been updated to v3.3 “SYNC”.

This time it is almost all about character settings: how you register a character’s images, and how they move. That part has been rebuilt.

💡 If you don’t use the character feature

You can skip this update. Nothing here changes any other part of making a video.

🗂️

Character assets, in three methods

Sequence / Animated image / Audio-driven. Switch between them with tabs, depending on what you want.

📦

Much easier to load

Folder picking, drag and drop, and ZIP files all work. Reordering is a drag too.

💬

The speech bubble follows

Move the character left or right and the bubble moves with it, by exactly the same amount.

CONTENTS
🗂️CHARACTER ASSETS“Icon images” are now “character assets”

The images you can register are sorted into three methods, so you can pick the one that fits.

Until now, the images you could register for a character were effectively treated as one undifferentiated pile. In practice several kinds of icon asset were already accepted, but that never really came across — so it has been sorted out.

In v3.3 there are three methods.

MethodWhat you supplyHow it movesWho it suits
SequenceSeveral still imagesCycles through them in the order you registered them while the character speaksWorks from a single image. The easiest option
Animated imageOne GIF, animated WebP or APNGLoops that animation while the character speaksPeople who already have animated assets
Audio-drivenExactly six still imagesThe mouth follows the narration volumeThe most natural, and the centrepiece of this release

🎨 Let another app build the assets

Assets for “Animated image” and “Audio-driven” can be generated with MouthLoop, the Gemini Canvas app covered further down.

The three tabs sit along the top of the settings screen. Click to switch.

💾EVERYDAY USESwitching methods doesn’t throw away what you had

Each of the three methods keeps its own assets, so trying one and going back costs you nothing.

This is a quiet but important detail. The assets for the three methods are stored separately.

  1. Add three images under “Sequence”Start with the easy method.
  2. Try “Audio-driven”Switch tabs and give the other method a go.
  3. Go back to “Sequence” after allYour original three images are still there.

✅ No need to load them again

You never have to re-upload assets just because you switched. Try things freely, and go back just as freely.

❓HELPThe “?” button explains the method you are on

Open the explanation for the method you have selected, only when you need it.

There is a small “?” button to the right of the three tabs.

Press it and only the explanation for the method you are on opens underneath. You are not made to read all three at once, so open it only when you are unsure.

📦LOADINGGetting assets in is far less work now

Folder picking, drag and drop, and ZIP files are all supported.

There are more ways to register assets now. Any of them will do.

  • Select several files (as before)
  • Select a whole folderNEW
  • Drag and drop (files or folders)IMPROVED
  • ZIP fileNEW

ZIP works for “Sequence” and “Audio-driven” (a single-file animation doesn’t need it). The contents are read in filename order, so if you number them 01.png–06.png they come in exactly in that order.

⚠️ There are limits, just in case

Go over one and a message appears, and the import stops there.

🖱️REORDERINGReorder by dragging

You can now drag a card straight to where you want it.

Registered images are laid out as cards. You can now drag a card directly to change the order. While you drag, a pink vertical line shows where it will land.

Before

You had to move things one position at a time with the “←/→” arrow buttons.

v3.3

Grab the card and drop it straight into the position you want.

🔁 The arrow buttons are still there

When you want to nudge something one step at a time, or dragging is awkward on your device, the old “←/→” buttons still work.

🎬THE BIG ONEAbout “Audio-driven” (six-image lip sync)

Six still images, switched in time with the volume of the narration.

This is the centrepiece of the release. Six still images are switched in time with the volume of the narration. Each of the six has a fixed role, and the name of that role is shown on its card.

  1. Eyes open, mouth closed (idle)
  2. Eyes open, mouth half open
  3. Eyes open, mouth open
  4. Eyes closed, mouth closed
  5. Eyes closed, mouth half open
  6. Eyes closed, mouth open

⚠️ The order matters

Add them in this order. If you are loading a ZIP, numbering the files 01–06 in this order is the reliable way to do it.

You can replace them one at a time

When it is only the fourth image you are unhappy with, hover over it and use the “Replace” button that appears to swap out just that one. There is no need to start over.

Leave the lip-sync interval on “Auto”

The speed the mouth moves at is set to “Auto” by default. For each line it estimates the speaking rate from the length of the audio and the amount of subtitle text, and matches it.

Try it on Auto first, and only switch to “Manual” and reach for the slider if it doesn’t feel right.

💬LAYOUTMove the character and the speech bubble follows

The icon and the bubble now keep their relative positions.

Before

Move the character icon left or right and the speech bubble stayed where it was, so you had to line it up by hand every time.

v3.3

Move the icon left or right and the bubble moves with it, by exactly the same amount.

The gap between the icon and the bubble — their relative positions — is preserved, so once you have arranged things nicely you can move the whole pair left and right together. You can of course still adjust the bubble on its own, exactly as before.

🪄COMPANION TOOLNow connected to “MouthLoop”, the Gemini Canvas app for building assets

A separate app that builds lip-sync assets automatically from a single image.

“Six lip-sync images? How am I supposed to make those?” — a fair question, so there is now a separate app for building the assets.

It is called MouthLoop, a standalone app that runs on Google Gemini Canvas. Give it one image and it builds the lip-sync assets for you.

  • Six PNGs (as a ZIP) → drop straight into “Audio-driven” in SlideCast Studio
  • An animated WebP → drops straight into “Animated image”

A “Build assets in MouthLoop” link is embedded in the character settings screen in SlideCast Studio, so you can open it from there. Download what you made and drag it straight in.

🍵WRAPPING UPIn closing

“Sequence” alone is perfectly usable. Move up to “Audio-driven” when you want to.

This update should land well if any of these is you.

  • You make videos where a character talks, and you want the mouth to look a little more natural
  • You have plenty of character images and registering them one at a time was tedious
  • You quietly resented fixing the speech bubble every time you moved the character

“Sequence” on its own is perfectly usable, and the idea is that you step up to “Audio-driven” when you start to care. So that you don’t have to produce six images up front, it works from two.

The unspoken theme of this release was moving the work of producing those six images out to a separate app. More tools is not usually a good thing, but “making images” and “assembling a video” ask different things of your head, so keeping them apart turns out to be faster.

If you hit a bug, or something here is hard to follow, a comment telling me so is a real help. Thank you, as always.
